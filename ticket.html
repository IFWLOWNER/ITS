Before you start: your data and ITS

This league uses ITS to manage stewarding. ITS stores your Discord username, tickets involving you, and stewarding decisions about you.

Shared with other ITS leagues: confirmed penalties and bans only, kept for 2 years from the decision and then deleted automatically.

Never shared: open tickets, reports that didn't lead to a penalty, and internal steward notes.

You can ask to see, correct or dispute your record, or have your data deleted, at any time from the Your data tab.
