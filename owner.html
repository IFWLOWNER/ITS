ITS Privacy & Data Rules for Leagues
Every league on ITS must follow these rules. Read them, add the clause to your league rules, then confirm at the bottom of this page.

1. How ITS uses driver data

ITS stores the following for each driver:

  • Discord ID and display name
  • Tickets raised by or about the driver
  • Stewarding decisions, penalties and bans

A Discord ID counts as personal data under UK GDPR, even without a real name attached, so these rules apply to everything above.

What is shared across leagues: confirmed penalties and bans are visible to the owners and staff of other leagues using ITS.

What is never shared across leagues: open or unresolved tickets, reports that did not lead to a penalty, and internal stewarding notes. These stay within your own league.

2. Who is responsible for what

ITS is responsible forYour league is responsible for
Running the system securelyTelling your members about ITS (see section 3)
Showing drivers a privacy notice inside ITSEntering only accurate, confirmed decisions

Deleting records when the retention period endsUsing other leagues' records responsibly
Keeping its ICO registration up to datePassing driver requests to ITS promptly
Publishing the ITS privacy policyLimiting ITS access to staff who need it

3. What your league must add to its rules

You must add the clause below to your league rules, and post it somewhere every member will see: your rules channel, your sign-up form, or a pinned message. We've filled in your league's details for you.

4. Confirming your members are informed

Before your league can use cross-league records, you must press the confirmation button below. ITS records the date and your Discord username. If the clause changes, you'll be asked to confirm again. Cross-league access stays switched off until confirmation is given — your league's penalties and bans aren't shared, and your staff can't see other leagues' records.

5. Accuracy and fair process

Because other leagues can see your decisions, they must be fair and accurate:

  • Log a penalty or ban only once the decision is final.
  • Give the driver a chance to put their side before deciding.
  • Record a clear reason for every penalty or ban.
  • Correct or remove a record quickly if it turns out to be wrong.
  • Keep notes factual. No insults, opinions about the person, or personal comments.

6. Using other leagues' records

Records from other leagues are there to help you make informed decisions, for example when a new driver applies to join. When you use them:

  • Treat them as information, not an automatic ban, unless your own rules say otherwise.
  • Do not copy, screenshot or share them outside ITS.
  • Never post another league's records publicly or use them to target or harass a driver.

7. Driver requests and data deletion

Drivers can ask to see their record, correct it, dispute a decision, or have their data deleted.

  • If a driver asks your league, pass the request to ITS within 3 days (use the form below).
  • ITS will respond to the driver within one month, as UK GDPR requires.

When a driver asks for deletion:

  • Their profile, open tickets and any records without a confirmed penalty are deleted.
  • Confirmed penalties and bans are kept for from the date of the decision so ITS leagues can protect their drivers. After that, they are deleted automatically.
  • The driver can still see and dispute these records during that time.

8. Breaking these rules

If a league breaks these rules, ITS may suspend its cross-league access or end its subscription.
