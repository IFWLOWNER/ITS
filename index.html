Drivers report in seconds

They sign in with Discord, add a video and submit from your league's own link. Named drivers give their side.

A proper stewarding panel

Stewards see evidence, statements and history in one place, recommend a penalty, and the Head Steward finalises.

Consistent penalties

Use the ITS Standard rulebook or your own. Every rule has Mild, Standard and Severe — similar incidents, similar outcomes.

Know who's joining

See a driver's confirmed penalties and bans from every ITS league — and get a heads-up when a banned driver turns up in yours.

Lives in your Discord

Every ticket posts to your channel and updates itself: New → Under review → Finalised. No channel spam.

Bring your old records

Moving from Excel or Google Sheets? Import your past outcomes so history isn't lost on day one.
